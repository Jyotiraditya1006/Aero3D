Neural Engine Architecture

A deep dive into the mathematical and AI pipelines powering the single-pass 3D reconstruction engine.

1. Telemetry & Optical Extraction

  • ■ Extracts H.265 video frames & decodes embedded DJI SRT subtitles.
  • ■ Synchronizes WGS84 GPS (Lat, Lon, Alt) to the exact millisecond.
  • ■ Computes Laplacian variance to automatically select the sharpest frames.

2. Zero-Shot Semantic Isolation

  • ■ Initializes `CIDAS/clipseg-rd64-refined` via HuggingFace Transformers.
  • ■ Generates pixel-level probability logits based on tactical text prompts.
  • ■ Mathematically deletes non-target background pixels (noise reduction up to 90%).

3. Neural Depth Estimation

  • ■ Feeds isolated frames into the `Depth Anything V2` Neural Network.
  • ■ Converts 2D RGB data into high-precision floating-point disparity tensors.
  • ■ Anchors Z-axis dynamically using true physical GPS altitude to prevent scale drift.

4. Coordinate Reprojection & Fusion

  • ■ Unprojects 2D pixels into 3D Camera Space using intrinsic matrix (fx, fy, cx, cy).
  • ■ Applies global rotation (R_enu) & translation (t_enu) to align geometry to the real world.
  • ■ Exports ultra-dense, million-point PLY clouds ready for C4ISR GIS analysis.
